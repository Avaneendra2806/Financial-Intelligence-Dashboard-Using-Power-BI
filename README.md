# 📊 Financial Intelligence Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Introduction

The **Financial Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze financial sales performance for the years **2013 and 2014**.

The project focuses on analyzing **sales, profit, profit margin, units sold, countries, products, customer segments, discounts, COGS, and monthly financial trends**.

The dashboard transforms financial sales data into actionable business insights using **Power Query, DAX, data modeling, KPI reporting, interactive filters, financial flow analysis, geographic visualization, and time-series analysis**.

The primary objective is to provide an **executive-level overview of sales, profitability, and business performance** and support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project provides an interactive financial performance dashboard covering the financial years **2013 and 2014**.

The dashboard analyzes:

- Total Sales

- Sales by Customer Segment
- Gross Sales
- Discounts
- Net Sales
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Monthly Sales Trends

- Monthly Profit Margin Trends

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- **Country**
- **Segment**
- **Product**
- **Discount Band**

# Business Questions & Answers

## 1. What is the overall financial performance of the business?

**Answer:**  
The business generated approximately **$118.73M in Total Sales** and **$16.89M in Total Profit**, with an overall **Profit Margin of 14.23%**. Approximately **1.13M units** were sold.

**Business Insight:**  
The business has a strong revenue base with significant sales volume across multiple countries, products, and customer segments.

---

## 2. Which country generated the highest sales?

**Answer:**  
**France** generated the highest sales at approximately **$24.35M**, contributing **20.51% of total sales**.

**Business Insight:**  
France is the strongest individual market and an important contributor to overall revenue.

## 6. Which customer segment generated the highest sales?

**Answer:**  
The **Government** segment generated approximately **$52.50M**, representing **44.22% of total sales**.

**Business Insight:**  
Government is the largest customer segment and a major driver of overall revenue.

---

## 7. How much do Government and Small Business contribute together?

**Answer:**  
Government contributes **44.22%** of sales, while Small Business contributes **35.74%**.

Together, they contribute approximately **80% of total sales**.

**Business Insight:**  
The company's revenue is highly concentrated in these two customer segments, making customer retention and segment diversification important.

---

## 8. How much revenue is reduced through discounts?

**Answer:**  
The business generated approximately **$127.93M in Gross Sales**. Discounts reduced Gross Sales by approximately **$9.21M**, resulting in approximately **$118.73M in Net Sales**.

**Business Insight:**  
Discounting has a meaningful impact on realized revenue, so discount strategies should be carefully evaluated.

---

## 9. How does COGS affect profitability?

**Answer:**  
The business generated approximately **$118.73M in Net Sales** and incurred approximately **$101.83M in COGS**, resulting in approximately **$16.89M in Profit**.

**Business Insight:**  
COGS is the largest cost component in the financial flow and has a significant impact on final profitability.
